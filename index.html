How CivicFix Works

A structured, four-step civic redressal pipeline connecting citizens directly with municipal response engineers.

1

1. Report

Submit your complaint in seconds with photos, locality tags, and severity level. Instant unique tracking ID issued.

2

2. Verification

The municipal ward control center verifies the issue and assigns it to the specialized engineering department.

3

3. Resolution

Ground maintenance crew is deployed with timestamped progress milestones visible to the citizen.

4

4. Improved

Quality check completed, complaint closed with photographic proof, and transparent public audit record updated.

About CivicFix Governance Platform

CivicFix is a modern citizen redressal architecture designed to eliminate bureaucratic delay and provide open, transparent resolution for essential urban civic services.

Citizen-Centric SLA

Guaranteed response timeframes based on severity: 12 hours for critical hazards (sparking lines, open manholes) and 48 hours for standard municipal repairs.

Direct Department Routing

Grievances bypass intermediate paperwork and are routed directly to junior engineers, electrical linemen, and solid waste compactor fleets.

Immutable Audit Trails

Every status transition from Submitted to Resolved logs the acting authority, date, and field report for complete democratic accountability.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I track my submitted complaint?

Once you submit a report, CivicFix generates a unique 10-character code (e.g. CF-2026-48291). You can input this code in the Track Complaint view anytime to check real-time officer notes and photo updates.

Can I delete or cancel a complaint?

Yes. Citizens can manage and remove their records from the Citizen Dashboard or the complaint details view.

How are priority levels handled?

Complaints marked Critical (such as sparking electrical transformers or missing manhole covers) trigger instant notifications at the ward emergency desk for immediate on-site barricading within 2 hours.

Where is my complaint data stored?

This demonstration portal uses the browser's persistent LocalStorage engine. All your reports, status changes, and filter preferences remain intact across page reloads.
